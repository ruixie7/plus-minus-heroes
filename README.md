**Plus & Minus Heroes**

Pick a hero (Pip the frog, Bun the bunny, or Rex the dino) and battle Pinch the crab, Grizzle the bear, or Blaze the dragon by answering addition and subtraction facts. Each right answer knocks a heart off the boss, and each wrong answer costs one of yours.

The game adapts to your child: two right answers in a row bring harder questions, and two wrong bring easier ones. Every fact remembers how well it’s known, and a “My facts” page shows which ones are mastered. Progress stays in the browser on that device.

It’s one index.html file with no build step. Tuning numbers (hearts, difficulty rules, number range) are in the CONFIG block at the top.

Inspired by Multiply Heroes by Zhongzhou.
